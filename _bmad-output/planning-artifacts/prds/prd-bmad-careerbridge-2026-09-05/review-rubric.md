# PRD Quality Review — CareerBridge (prd-bmad-careerbridge-2026-09-05)

Calibration: graded Iteration 1 requirements deliverable, five-student course team, instructor as customer, chain-top for use-case analysis, wireframes, architecture, database design, GitHub Issues, and Jest tests keyed on FR IDs. Stakes: internal / course project. The author's deliberate shape choices (FRs by fifteen use cases with stable IDs, one-sentence FRs, separate NFRs, tech in the addendum, journeys as a table, assumptions as a locator plus addendum table, under 3,000 words) are taken as given and judged on execution only.

## Overall verdict

This is a disciplined capability spec that mostly delivers what §0 promises: 62 one-sentence FRs under fifteen use cases with contiguous IDs, eleven NFRs that carry numbers rather than adjectives, an honest Non-Goals list, a counter-metric, and a 31-entry assumption set whose inline / locator / addendum roundtrip is clean. What is at risk is the claim that "each is checkable by one automated test": four FRs bundle three or four independently testable behaviors (FR-A5-1, FR-R2-4, FR-A3-5, FR-R2-5), two rules contradict the document's own non-goals or assumptions (rejecting at Stage Offer versus A-31 "cannot be rescinded"; A-20 "existing Applications continue" versus FR-R4-4's freeze), and the glossary is missing the two nouns the Iteration 2 data model will need first (Account and its per-role status). Ready for use-case analysis and wireframes now; needs a short fix pass before it is the frozen test oracle §0 and NFR-10 describe.

## Decision-readiness — adequate

Decisions are stated as decisions, not considerations: the fixed pipeline and full state model in §3, one Resume per Applicant, in-app-only notifications (FR-X-3), single Administrator "as written" with option B costed in the addendum, default cap 5. The six Open Questions in §8 are genuinely open and each names who settles it or which assumption it reopens. The Non-Goals in §6 name what was given up rather than only what was chosen.

Where the PRD dodges is in tensions between its own rules. Expiry is the clearest: FR-M2-4 says existing Applications "keep their Stage", FR-R4-4 (A-15) says an Application to a non-Live Posting can only be rejected, and the addendum's A-20 line says "existing Applications continue." Read together, an Applicant sitting in Stage Interview the day a Posting expires can never receive an Offer and can only be rejected — a consequence nobody has stated or chosen. Similarly, nothing bounds how many Applications may be in Stage Offer at once for one Posting, yet FR-R6-3 silently auto-rejects the other offerees with "Position filled" when the first accepts. Those are decisions the PRD has made by omission rather than surfaced. The cap question (§8 item 2) is stated without its stakes, although the brief's memlog recorded exactly why it matters.

### Findings

- **high** Expiry freezes the pipeline without saying so (FR-M2-4, FR-R4-4, addendum A-20) — FR-M2-4 keeps existing Applications at their Stage, FR-R4-4 then "refuses a new Stage change ... except rejection", so a Screening or Interview Application at an Expired Posting has exactly one possible future: Rejected. The addendum's A-20 wording "existing Applications continue" reads as the opposite. *Fix:* decide once — either "a Recruiter may still advance and offer on Applications to an Expired Posting; expiry stops intake only" (change FR-R4-4 to Filled/Closed only) or "expiry ends the pipeline; remaining Applications must be rejected or left" — state it in FR-R4-4 and rewrite A-20's one-liner to match.
- **medium** Concurrent Offers per Posting decided by omission (FR-R6-1, FR-R6-3, A-18) — FR-R6-1 lets a Recruiter extend an offer on any Interview-stage Application with no limit; FR-R6-3 then auto-rejects "every other Active Application" (Offer is Active per §3) with "Position filled" when one accepts, so a second offeree is rejected by a system event with no human decision, contrary to the UJ-2 promise that rejections carry a Recruiter's reason. *Fix:* add "at most one Application per Posting may be in Stage Offer at a time" to FR-R6-1 (or explicitly allow multiple offers and say the "Position filled" rejection is the intended outcome), and add it to §8 if the team wants Dr. Ren's view.
- **medium** Cap question stated without its stakes (§8 item 2, FR-M4-1) — "Default Application Cap of 5 needs the customer's confirmation (Q-004)" omits the reason it needs confirming: a cap of 5 cuts against the niche of an early-career applicant applying broadly, which the brief coaching flagged. A customer reading the question cold will just say "yes, 5". *Fix:* one clause: "(Q-004; a cap of 5 conflicts with the early-career applicant who applies to 15–20 Postings — confirm 5, or raise the default)".
- **low** Vision defers the "why" entirely to the brief (§1) — "Problem, niche, and the two semester outcomes ... are in the brief." Acceptable for a chain document, but a grader or teammate reading only the PRD gets the mechanism ("one fixed pipeline that both sides can see in full") without the problem it solves. *Fix:* one sentence naming the pain (applicants hear nothing; small employers track in spreadsheets) before the mechanism.

## Substance over theater — strong

No persona theater: three roles in one table, each with a one-line "success looks like" that later FRs actually honor (Applicant "always knows the Stage and outcome" maps to FR-A4-2, FR-A5-2; Administrator "controls ... the business rules" maps to UC-M4). Named protagonists Sam, Maria, Devon are carried from the brief's demo script and used in UJ-1 and UJ-2. No differentiation section, no claimed novelty. The Vision is specific to this product ("multi-company job board for early-career applicants and small employers with no applicant tracking system", "one profile and one resume", "one fixed pipeline that both sides can see in full") and could not be swapped into a generic job-board PRD unchanged.

NFRs are largely earned: NFR-6 carries 2 s / 1,000 Postings / 10,000 Applications / 25 users; NFR-7 explicitly declines an uptime target; NFR-9 lists three checkable rules; NFR-10 ties test names to FR IDs. Success Criteria validate the semester's two theses (demo, traceability) and SC-C1 is a real counter-metric ("must not grow to look thorough").

### Findings

- **low** NFR-11 reads as course-rubric furniture (§5 NFR-11) — "separate presentation, business logic, persistence, and data layers" names four layers where "persistence" and "data" are not distinguishable, and the consequence "touches one layer" is a code-review judgment, not a test. *Fix:* name the layers the architecture will actually have (three is typical: UI, service, repository) and give a checkable rule, e.g. "no SQL outside the repository module (lint rule)".

## Strategic coherence — strong

The thesis is present and consistent: transparency of a fixed pipeline for two under-served roles, with the semester bet being a ten-minute demo plus a traceable process. Scope is problem-solving for a demo, and the iteration mapping follows the demo rather than what is easy: Iteration 1 is browse / post / approve (the spine of UJ-1), Iteration 2 is the pipeline, Iteration 3 is notifications, offers, and administration. UJ-3 turns the privacy and business-rule requirements into demonstrable proof points. SC-1 to SC-3 validate the thesis; SC-4 is process, but that is what the course grades. Non-Goals keep the arc narrow ("AI features of any kind unless all fifteen use cases pass their tests first").

### Findings

- **medium** SC-1's validation range does not resolve (§7 SC-1) — "Validates FR-A2-1 through FR-R6-3" implies a linear ID order the document does not define; taken in document order it excludes FR-A1-1 (Maria "registers"), FR-M1-1 and FR-M2-2 (both tagged "Realizes UJ-1"), and includes UC-A4, which no journey touches. *Fix:* "Validates every FR tagged Realizes UJ-1 and UJ-2" and rely on the tags (see the tag-coverage finding under Downstream usability).
- **low** Size letters read as MoSCoW without a legend (§4 headings, e.g. "UC-R4 ... (L, Iteration 2)") — "Size and iteration follow the brief" is the only hint that M/S/L are sizes, not Must/Should. A GitHub Issue author will label UC-R4 "Large" or "Low" depending on mood. *Fix:* "(size S/M/L, iteration)" in the §4 preamble.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

The bar the author set is "each is checkable by one automated test" (§0). About 50 of the 62 FRs meet it cleanly: FR-A3-4 (refuse at cap, show count and cap), FR-R4-3 (refuse backward, skipped, terminal), FR-A4-3 (withdraw → Withdrawn, stops counting), FR-M4-1 (cap 1–20 applies to later submissions), and the entire UC-M1 / UC-M2 group are model one-sentence, one-test requirements. Preconditions are stated as roles and statuses from the glossary, which is what makes them testable. The blanket refusal rule in the §4 preamble plus FR-X-2 saves every FR from restating authorization.

The remainder falls into three groups. First, FRs that stitch several independently testable behaviors into one sentence with "and" — most visibly FR-A5-1 (four behaviors) and FR-R2-4 (three). Second, rules that contradict another part of the document — FR-R4-2 permits rejection "in any active Stage", and Offer is Active, so a Recruiter can reject an Applicant who holds an offer, which is precisely the rescinding that §6 and A-31 say is not built. Third, a small number of gaps a test writer would trip on immediately: FR-A3-3 is silent on re-applying after Declined; FR-A5-2 says "on every Stage change" and then lists an event (interview recorded) that is not a Stage change. NFRs are almost all bounded; NFR-8's "completes each UJ-1 task without training" is the one adjective-grade sentence.

### Findings

- **high** FR-R4-2 contradicts §6 and A-31 (FR-R4-2, §6, addendum A-31) — "reject an Application in any active Stage" includes Stage Offer (§3: "Active Application — Stage Applied, Screening, Interview, or Offer"). Rejecting at Offer is rescinding the offer, which §6 lists as not built and A-31 states as "cannot be rescinded this semester". A Jest test for FR-R4-2 at Offer and a test for A-31 cannot both pass. Note FR-R6-3's cascade also rejects Offer-stage Applications. *Fix:* either "in Stage Applied, Screening, or Interview" in FR-R4-2 and an explicit carve-out in FR-R6-3 ("including any in Stage Offer"), or drop "rescinding" from §6/A-31 and accept that rejection at Offer is the rescind path.
- **high** FR-A5-1 bundles four behaviors (FR-A5-1) — unread count "on every page", list "newest first", "mark each as read", "follow each to the Application or Posting it concerns" are four tests across UI and API; a single `FR-A5-1` test name will hide which one failed. *Fix:* split: FR-A5-1 unread count on every page; FR-A5-2 open list newest first and mark read; FR-A5-3 each Notification links to its Application or Posting; renumber the rest of UC-A5.
- **high** FR-R2-4 bundles three behaviors (FR-R2-4) — "can edit the description and requirements of a Live Posting without re-approval", "is refused any change to its title, category, location, or expiry date", and "can close it manually, which moves it to Closed and removes it from the job list" are three independent tests, and closing is a distinct state transition that the architecture will treat separately. *Fix:* FR-R2-4 = editable fields on Live (positive and refused in one property test); new FR-R2-6 = manual close.
- **medium** FR-A3-5 bundles three (FR-A3-5) — "starts in Stage Applied", "keeps the Resume exactly as it was at submission", and "cannot be edited by the Applicant afterwards" are three facts with three fixtures (the snapshot needs a re-upload, the immutability needs an edit attempt). *Fix:* move "starts in Stage Applied" into FR-A3-1's consequence; keep FR-A3-5 as the snapshot rule; make immutability its own FR or fold it into FR-A4-4-style refusal.
- **medium** FR-R2-5 bundles list and resubmit (FR-R2-5) — a listing requirement ("list all of their Organization's Postings with status and ... the Administrator's reason") and a state transition ("edit and resubmit a Rejected Posting, which returns it to Pending Approval") in one sentence. *Fix:* split; the resubmit belongs beside FR-R2-3.
- **medium** FR-A3-3 leaves Declined unspecified (FR-A3-3, FR-A5-4) — the rule enumerates Active and Rejected (refuse) and Withdrawn (allow), but FR-A5-4 leaves the Posting Live after Declined, so "may Devon re-apply after declining?" has no answer. *Fix:* add Declined to one side of the rule (A-5 in the addendum should say which).
- **medium** FR-A5-2 event list is not a Stage-change list (FR-A5-2) — "on every Stage change ... including ... the interview date when one is recorded": recording an interview (FR-R5-1) is not a Stage change; and "every Stage change" literally includes changes the Applicant made themselves (withdraw, accept, decline). *Fix:* enumerate the events: advance, rejection (with reason), offer, interview recorded, "Position filled"; exclude self-initiated changes.
- **medium** NFR-8 first clause has no bound (§5 NFR-8) — "A first-time user completes each UJ-1 task without training" gives no number of users, success rate, or time; it cannot fail. The browser and 375 px clauses are fine. *Fix:* either bound it ("3 of 3 first-time testers complete UJ-1's applicant tasks unaided in under 5 minutes, recorded before Iteration 3") or drop the clause and keep the compatibility bounds.
- **low** Smaller bundles and unspecified inputs (FR-A1-1, FR-A1-3, FR-M4-2, FR-X-1, FR-A2-3, FR-R5-2) — FR-A1-1 combines email-uniqueness refusal with auto-login and gives no password rule, so no refusal test exists; FR-A1-3 combines type/size validation with replace-on-reupload; FR-M4-2 is add + rename + retire; FR-X-1 is login + logout + generic message; FR-A2-3 does not say whether keyword match is case-insensitive substring; FR-R5-2 does not say whether an interview must have been recorded (FR-R5-1) before an outcome. *Fix:* accept the bundles as "one test with N cases" and add a minimum password length to FR-A1-1, "case-insensitive substring" to FR-A2-3, and a precondition to FR-R5-2.
- **low** NFR-6 lacks a measurement point (§5 NFR-6) — "respond within 2 seconds" without percentile or where measured. *Fix:* "server response p95 under 2 s, measured in the CI performance test".

## Scope honesty — strong

§6 does real work: eight named omissions, each phrased so it cannot be assumed in (e.g. "a Recruiter in more than one Organization", "offer expiry, rescinding, or negotiation"). Thirty-one `(A-n)` marks with a locator and a full addendum table, plus §8's six Open Questions, is the right density for a draft whose customer will "confirm or correct" — none of the assumptions is disguised as a fact, and §9 even ranks which reversals would ripple ("A-6, A-13, A-18 with A-19, A-20, A-31"). The addendum records which assumptions were overturned in the team review, which is more honest than most PRDs manage. De-scoping is planned (iteration mapping, slip order) rather than done silently.

### Findings

- **medium** Applicants cannot open a non-Live Posting they applied to (FR-A2-2, FR-A4-1, FR-A4-2, FR-A5-1) — FR-A2-2 grants detail-page access only for a Live Posting; once it is Filled, Expired, or Closed, the only FR-granted view an Applicant has is the title in FR-A4-1, yet FR-A5-1 says a Notification leads "to the Application or Posting it concerns". *Fix:* either extend FR-A2-2 ("and an Applicant can open any Posting they have an Application to") or have FR-A4-2 show the Posting's details as of submission.
- **low** Silent omissions the reader must infer (§6) — password reset / forgotten password; deleting an account (§8 item 3 mentions "deleted" but no FR deletes anything); an Administrator editing or removing a Posting or Organization directly; deleting a Rejected Posting (FR-R2-2 covers Draft and Pending only). *Fix:* add the first three to §6 and the last to FR-R2-2.
- **low** §9 overstates "each is stated in full where it is marked" (§9, addendum table A-12, A-21, A-28, A-29) — the addendum's rule text carries content absent from the marked FR: A-12 "category and location from Reference Data" is not in FR-R2-1; A-21 "'Reassign' means ..." uses a word that does not appear in the PRD; A-28 "no native apps" and A-29 "no formal WCAG audit" are not in NFR-8 / NFR-9. *Fix:* fold the four clauses into the FRs (they are short) or soften §9 to "summarized".

## Downstream usability — adequate

ID hygiene is excellent: every FR ID is unique and contiguous within its use case, NFR-1 to NFR-11 and A-1 to A-31 are contiguous, every `(A-n)` appears inline, in the §9 locator, and in the addendum table with matching FR locations (one wrinkle noted under Mechanical). All UJ, FR, NFR, SC, Q-nnn and D-nnn references resolve (Q-004, Q-011, Q-012 to `inputs/QUESTIONS.md`; D-004, D-005 to the brief). The addendum's screen inventory and iteration mapping are exactly what the wireframe and Issue authors need, and the traceability convention makes NFR-10 mechanical.

Two things will hurt Iteration 2. The glossary defines roles but not the login entity: "account" is used 11 times (FR-A1-1, UC-R1, all of UC-M3) and never defined, "Suspended" is listed only under Recruiter although FR-M3-2 suspends any account, and Applicant and Administrator have no status at all — the database design will have to invent the account-status enum. And the "Realizes UJ-n" tags, which the use-case analysis will use to reconstruct the demo script, are incomplete.

### Findings

- **medium** Glossary lacks Account and per-role status (§3, FR-A1-1, FR-M3-1 to FR-M3-5, NFR-3) — "account" is the thing FR-M3-1 searches, FR-M3-2 suspends, FR-M3-4 re-roles, and FR-X-1 logs in, but §3 has no entry; Suspended is scoped to Recruiter only; Applicant and Administrator statuses are undefined, so FR-M3-2 applied to an Applicant has no glossary state to land in. *Fix:* add "**Account** — one login (email, password) with exactly one role; status Active or Suspended; a Recruiter Account additionally has Pending Approval or Rejected" and remove "plus Suspended" from the Recruiter entry.
- **medium** "Realizes UJ-n" coverage is incomplete and once misplaced (§2, §4) — UJ-1 says Maria "registers, uploads a Resume" and "is notified", but FR-A1-1, FR-A1-3, and FR-A5-2 (for UJ-1) carry no tag; UJ-2 says Devon "reads it", but FR-A4-2 carries no tag; FR-M1-1 is tagged UJ-1 though UJ-1 contains no Recruiter-approval step (Sam is already approved; FR-M2-2 is the approval in the path). *Fix:* tag FR-A1-1, FR-A1-3, FR-A5-2 (UJ-1 and UJ-2), FR-A4-2 (UJ-2); either drop the tag on FR-M1-1 or add "the Administrator approves Sam" to UJ-1.
- **low** "job list" and "detail page" are undefined nouns (§3; FR-A2-1, FR-A2-3, FR-R2-4, FR-R6-2, FR-M2-4, FR-X-2, addendum screen inventory) — used seven times and load-bearing (three FRs say a Posting "leaves the job list"), but the glossary has only Posting. *Fix:* add "**Job List** — the public list of Live Postings (FR-A2-1)" and "**Job Detail** — the public page of one Live Posting (FR-A2-2)", or replace with "the public Posting list".
- **low** "employment type" has no domain (FR-R2-1, FR-A2-2) — a Posting field that is neither Reference Data (§3 lists categories, locations, Stage labels only) nor given values, so the schema cannot decide enum versus free text. *Fix:* either add it to Reference Data or list the values ("Full-time, Part-time, Internship, Contract").
- **low** §4 is not standalone on sizing (§4 preamble) — "Size and iteration follow the brief" sends the Issue author to another document for the S/M/L meaning. *Fix:* see the legend finding under Strategic coherence.

## Shape fit — strong

Capability spec grouped by use cases with a three-row journey table, three roles, and tech pushed to the addendum is the right shape for a multi-role product built by five students for a demo. UJs are not overhead here: the demo script is the grading event, and UJ-1/UJ-2 with named protagonists give the wireframe and test authors a concrete script; UJ-3 as proof points turns three otherwise-invisible requirements (privacy, cap, rule change) into something demonstrable. Not over-formalized (no persona pages, no market section, no `[NOTE FOR PM]` scaffolding the audience would not use). The addendum is properly scoped — mechanism options are framed as "architecture decides" rather than pre-empting Iteration 2. Length lands on the author's target: PRD body ≈2,998 words with markup stripped (3,110 raw), §4 + §5 ≈2,100 words, roughly three pages of requirements. No findings.
